

## 🗺️ 2. Network Topology

### 2.1 Devices Used
List all devices added to your Packet Tracer workspace.

1. Router – Used to connect different networks and manage data traffic.

2. Switch – Used to connect multiple devices within the same local area network (LAN).

3. PCs (Computers) – Used as end devices to send and receive data.

4. Server – Used to provide network services and share resources.

Copper Straight-Through Cables – Used to connect the computers and server to the switch, and the switch to the router.
